Our Story

Who We Are

Nexora IoT Solutions was founded in 2025 in Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh, with a clear mission: to make enterprise-grade IoT technology accessible to businesses of all sizes.

We believe in the transformative power of connected systems — and we're committed to building solutions that are not just technologically advanced, but genuinely useful and reliable for the businesses that depend on them.

  • Headquartered in Rajahmundry

    Proudly local, thinking global. We serve clients across Andhra Pradesh and beyond.

  • Established in 2025

    A young company with ambitious goals and a relentless drive for innovation.

  • Client-First Philosophy

Our Services

Comprehensive IoT solutions engineered for enterprise scale, reliability, and measurable ROI.

Full Service Portfolio

Everything You Need to Go Connected

From initial consultation to full deployment and ongoing support, Nexora handles every layer of your IoT journey.

01
Device Integration & Management

Comprehensive onboarding of sensors, actuators, gateways, and edge devices. Multi-vendor support, firmware management, and unified device registry.

02
Cloud Connectivity Platform

Scalable cloud infrastructure with support for MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, WebSocket, and LwM2M protocols. Multi-cloud and hybrid deployment options.

03
AI & Predictive Analytics

Machine learning models for anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, and operational optimization. Custom dashboards and real-time alerting.

04
IoT Cybersecurity

Zero-trust architecture, end-to-end encryption, certificate management, intrusion detection, and compliance with industry standards.

05
Industrial IoT (IIoT)

Ruggedized solutions for manufacturing plants, energy facilities, and logistics hubs. SCADA integration, OPC-UA, and Modbus support.

06
Smart Building & Automation

Building management systems, environmental monitoring, energy optimization, and occupancy analytics for commercial real estate.

07
Managed IoT Services

Fully managed operations with 24/7 monitoring, proactive maintenance, SLA-backed uptime guarantees, and dedicated support engineers.

08
Custom IoT Development

Bespoke hardware-software co-design, embedded firmware, custom protocols, and proprietary platform development for unique use cases.

09
IoT Consulting & Training

Strategic roadmap consulting, technology assessments, and hands-on training programs to upskill your internal teams.
