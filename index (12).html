Our Services

Comprehensive IoT solutions engineered for enterprise scale, reliability, and measurable ROI.

Full Service Portfolio

Everything You Need to Go Connected

From initial consultation to full deployment and ongoing support, Nexora handles every layer of your IoT journey.

01
Device Integration & Management

Comprehensive onboarding of sensors, actuators, gateways, and edge devices. Multi-vendor support, firmware management, and unified device registry.

02
Cloud Connectivity Platform

Scalable cloud infrastructure with support for MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, WebSocket, and LwM2M protocols. Multi-cloud and hybrid deployment options.

03
AI & Predictive Analytics

Machine learning models for anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, and operational optimization. Custom dashboards and real-time alerting.

04
IoT Cybersecurity

Zero-trust architecture, end-to-end encryption, certificate management, intrusion detection, and compliance with industry standards.

05
Industrial IoT (IIoT)

Ruggedized solutions for manufacturing plants, energy facilities, and logistics hubs. SCADA integration, OPC-UA, and Modbus support.

06
Smart Building & Automation

Building management systems, environmental monitoring, energy optimization, and occupancy analytics for commercial real estate.

07
Managed IoT Services

Fully managed operations with 24/7 monitoring, proactive maintenance, SLA-backed uptime guarantees, and dedicated support engineers.

08
Custom IoT Development

Bespoke hardware-software co-design, embedded firmware, custom protocols, and proprietary platform development for unique use cases.

09
IoT Consulting & Training

Strategic roadmap consulting, technology assessments, and hands-on training programs to upskill your internal teams.

Software Services

Custom Software Solutions

Beyond hardware and connectivity — we build the software layer that makes your IoT ecosystem truly intelligent.

01
IoT Mobile Applications

Cross-platform iOS & Android apps for real-time device monitoring, control, notifications, and analytics — built with React Native and Flutter.

02
Web Dashboard & Portals

Fully responsive web dashboards with live data visualization, role-based access control, and white-label options for your brand.

03
Embedded Firmware Development

Low-level firmware for microcontrollers (ESP32, STM32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi) with OTA update support and power optimization.

04
API & System Integration

RESTful and GraphQL APIs to connect your IoT platform with ERP, CRM, SCADA, and third-party enterprise software ecosystems.

05
Data Engineering & Storage

Time-series databases, data pipelines, ETL workflows, and long-term archival solutions optimized for high-volume IoT telemetry.

06
AI-Powered Automation

Rule engines, intelligent alerting, automated workflows, and ML inference at the edge — reducing human intervention and operational costs.

Who We Train

Programs for Every Audience

We tailor our workshops and training content to suit the background and goals of each audience.

🎓
Engineering Colleges

Semester-long courses, final-year project guidance, lab workshops, and guest lectures on IoT architecture, embedded systems, and cloud platforms. Curriculum aligned with university syllabi.

🏫
Schools & Polytechnics

Age-appropriate introductory IoT workshops using Arduino and Raspberry Pi. We introduce students to sensors, basic programming, and smart devices through engaging, hands-on activities.

🏢
Corporates & Organizations

Professional upskilling programs for technical and non-technical employees. Topics include IoT strategy, platform management, data analytics, and integration with existing enterprise systems.

🏛️
Government Bodies

Awareness and capacity-building programs for government departments exploring smart city, agriculture, and infrastructure monitoring use cases.

💼
Individual Professionals

Weekend and online cohort-based programs for working professionals seeking to transition into IoT, embedded systems, or AI/ML domains with industry-recognized certificates.

🚀
Startups & Incubators

Bootcamp-style intensive workshops for founders and early-stage teams looking to build IoT products — covering hardware prototyping, MVP development, and go-to-market strategy.

What We Teach
